For recent work, start with paper 8 for gradient-based dimensional synthesis, then paper 11 for Fourier-based construction and timing assignment. Paper 8 tests fixed topologies including a Theo Jansen linkage; it is local optimization, not automatic topology discovery. Paper 11 is limited to four-bar synthesis, so its geometric insights are more portable than its full derivation.

Paper 10 uses direct search and genetic algorithms, including a multiobjective formulation; it does not require a trained model. Its second objective concerns the environmental cost of new parts, and its parts inventory is discrete, so adapting it to CP1's total-link-length objective requires changes. Paper 9 is useful for optimizer comparisons: its relay stabilizes the DE-PSO hybrid but matches standalone DE in the reported ablation rather than establishing an accuracy improvement. Paper 12 provides another recent curve-descriptor approach; only the publisher preview was inspected.

Keep LinkEdit Sections 3-4 and NSGA-II as foundations. SMS-EMOA connects directly to CP1's hypervolume score. Always validate any alternative curve descriptor against CP1's supplied scorer, including its treatment of scale.
